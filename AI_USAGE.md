# AI Usage Disclosure

This project was developed with the assistance of **Antigravity**, a powerful agentic AI coding assistant designed by the Google DeepMind team.

## How AI Was Used

1.  **Project Scaffolding**: Antigravity helped in initializing the Next.js (FE) and NestJS (BE) project structures, ensuring best practices and modern boilerplate configurations.
2.  **UI/UX Design**: The "Premium Aesthetics" of the landing page and multi-step registration wizard were crafted with AI guidance, utilizing Material UI's theming and advanced CSS-in-JS (Emotion) patterns.
3.  **Logic and State Management**: The hook-driven architecture (e.g., `useRegistration`, `useVerification`, `useProfileStep`) was implemented to separate business logic from the UI layer.
